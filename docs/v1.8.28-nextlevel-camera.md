# SpektraFilm iOS 16.5.1 v1.8.28 Build53 — NextLevel standalone camera

## Scope

- Import MIT-licensed NextLevel 0.19.1 through XcodeGen Swift Package Manager.
  Swift 6 / Xcode 16+ package, while the application target stays Swift 5 and
  iOS 16.5 minimum, supporting iOS 16.5.1.
- A toolbar camera button launches a full-screen independent capture UI.
  Live NextLevel preview, photo shutter, front/back camera and flash control.
- Camera authorization is requested only for video/camera hardware; no
  microphone usage is required because captureMode is photo only.
- Camera output is original high-resolution JPEG Data, forwarded directly to
  PhotoEditorModel.loadCapturedPhoto, the existing loadPhoto decoder and
  existing source-image film/paper preview and export path.
- The active film and paper choices persist. On cancel or error, the prior
  editor source photo remains unchanged. No automatic photo-library write.
- The NextLevel 0.19.1 isRawCaptureEnabled implementation is unfinished.
  RAW/Apple ProRAW are explicitly DISABLED in this camera version and not
  advertised as working.
- Source optical algorithms, original Metal diagnostic, float precision gates
  and strict release certification remain unchanged. An unsigned experimental
  IPA is not a physically verified capture workflow until tested on iPhone.

## Physical-device checks

1. On iOS 16.5.1, open camera and grant access. Wait for live preview.
2. Photograph with the rear camera: editor reappears with captured image,
   still selecting the same film and paper, and processed preview updates.
3. Reopen camera; verify front-facing capture, orientation and focus.
4. Try flash on compatible rear camera hardware.
5. Deny camera permission and confirm an actionable error; cancel must not
   remove the previously imported source image.
6. Verify original-size JPEG export and save-to-Photos, without cropping or
   forced downsampling beyond explicit export mode.
7. Re-run original Metal 8/8 and full-image physics validation independently.
