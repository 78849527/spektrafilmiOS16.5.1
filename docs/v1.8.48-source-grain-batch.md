  1. Original measured 256-point Float64 film H-D 3×3 sublayer inversion, `scipy.stats.poisson.rvs` followed by `scipy.stats.binom.rvs` for **all pixels** per channel and sublayer, seeded `channel+10*sublayer`. No Poisson thinning surrogate.
