# SpektraFilm iOS v1.8.34 Build 59 — edge drawers / three-row adjustment panel

UI only. Based on v1.8.33 and preserving original Python -> Swift/Metal source
precision, iOS 16.5 compatibility and numeric certification gates.

## Layout and interaction

- Film/paper: collapses to a narrow LEFT-side tab. Tap
  that tab to expand from the left edge. Tap the chevron on
  the expanded panel header to snap back to the left.
- Adjustment: collapses to a narrow RIGHT-side tab. Tap
  the tab to expand from the right. Tap the chevron on
  its header to snap back to the right.
- Adjustment open width is the available full screen; its height matches
  the expanded film/paper drawer height (up to 326 pt). The film panel
  retains a compact 168–204 pt width for wheel usability.
- Independent drawers may overlap. The most recently opened drawer
  has zIndex=2; the previously opened drawer stays underneath
  with zIndex=1. No automatic closing of the other drawer.
- Both are located 63 pt above the bottom main menu. The status/report
  chip and upward/top-aligned preview from v1.8.33 are unchanged.

## Adjustment controls — three rows

Row 1: single horizontal scroll row of short/tappable switches.
The switch uses a 28x16 pt track instead of full-width native Toggle:
64-bit Hanatos, scanning, scanner finishing, enlarger, preflash, s023,
emulsion scatter, back-reflection halation, DIR and density grain.
All control values, enabled rules, effects and refresh calls are retained.
Switch tap also selects its corresponding secondary category.

Row 2: horizontal secondary category selection:
  基础 / 扫描 / 印相 / 光学 / DIR / 颗粒

Row 3: scrolls parameters for just the selected category:
- 基础: source precision status, negative/reversal print exposure and LUT
  strength. Numeric-tap and double-name reset unchanged.
- 扫描: CAM16, black/white calibration, exposure references, sharpness.
- 印相: CMY source print optics, exposure normalization, compensation,
  preflash channels and s023 HD morphology.
- 光学: halation strength and source physical optical explanation.
- DIR: original FIR/YVV, 8-bit direct pixel IO, full physical optics,
  original camera metering/boost/lens, source emulsion scatter and scanner
  full-frame Double optics. The complete nested advanced settings remain.
- 颗粒: original three sublayers, stochastic Metal/SciPy, Poisson,
  grain strength and original A15 Metal diagnostics button.

The former '应用模拟' bottom action is reachable at the adjustment drawer
header's wand button, not buried below 500 pt of settings. All other
camera, film/paper and export buttons unchanged.

## Validation and safety

CI checks drawer overlay ordering, left/right tap-only controls, unchanged
full-width adjustment and safe toolbar offset, six detail categories,
compact 28x16 controls, native sliders and original reset gestures.
Full iPhoneOS compilation plus Python/Swift source tests required to
issue a build. An experimental unsigned IPA may be published with
independent full iPhone numeric/Numba/RAW precision guards not yet passed.
No automatic scientific equivalence or full photo numerical certificate.

## Tap-only safety revision
Both side handles remain visible and are 38×94pt buttons when closed.
Tap a handle to expand; tap the respective chevron button on the panel
header to snap it back. No swipe or drag gestures are attached to drawer
handles or headers. The separate photo before/after comparison gesture
and native film/paper Picker wheel scroll gestures are unchanged.
